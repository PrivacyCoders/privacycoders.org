Mission

Our mission is to design and maintain defensive tools that are usable in high-risk environments, then share field-ready playbooks that improve operational resilience and digital safety.

We prioritize secure defaults, low cognitive load, and transparent documentation so non-specialists can adopt strong practices quickly.
